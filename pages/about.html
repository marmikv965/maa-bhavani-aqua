About Maa Bhavani Aqua Solutions

Purity Powered by Trust — your dependable partner for water treatment products across India.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Quality

Maa Bhavani Aqua Solutions was founded with a single goal: to make genuine, high-performance water treatment products accessible to industries across India. From RO membranes to specialty chemicals, we source directly from certified manufacturers and deliver with speed and reliability.

Today, we serve pharmaceutical plants, power stations, hotels, hospitals and manufacturing units nationwide — backed by a team that understands water chemistry as well as it understands customer service.
